Porch Light icon

The Lantern Beam concept, finished on the macOS icon grid: a porch lantern on a short chain, a candle flame behind warm glass, and a cone of light pouring onto a soft pool on the floor. Every picture here is the PNG that ships in the app, embedded as is.

How it is built

  • Grid: 1024 canvas, body 824 x 824 at (100,100) with continuous corners (radius 185), drop shadow black 30 %, 12 px down, 12 px blur, inside the margin. Source: docs/design/porch-light-icon.svg.
  • 16 and 32 px: docs/design/porch-light-icon-small.svg, drawn on a 32 unit grid with edges on even units, body 24 at (4,4), so the lantern, glass, cone and pool land on whole pixels at both sizes. No chain links, two-stop gradients only.
  • 64 px is rendered from the full drawing. It keeps the chain, roof and flame and matches 128 px and up; the small drawing doubled to 64 px looks blocky.
  • Menu bar glyph: 18 x 18 pt, 16 pt tall with 1 pt above and below, like system menu bar icons. Two drawings of the same lantern: docs/design/porch-light-menubar.svg for the 36 px (2x) PNG, with 1 pt strokes on whole pixels, and docs/design/porch-light-menubar-1x.svg, drawn pixel by pixel for the 18 px (1x) PNG, so the hanger and the flame stay crisp on the 1x desk monitors. Imageset MenuBarGlyph, template rendering.
  • Render everything with Scripts/render-icons.sh; it checks each PNG's size with sips.
